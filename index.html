PAINTER & DECORATOR · LIMERICK

AS Painting

Personal, high-quality painting and decorating for homes. Based in Caherdavin, Limerick, Ahmed is happy to travel roughly 50–60 kilometres or about one hour to work on your project.

“Quality remains long after cost is forgotten.”

It echoes the well-known idea often associated with Aldo Gucci: quality is remembered long after price is forgotten. The point is simple — workmanship should still feel worthwhile after the invoice is long out of mind.

AS Painting’s idea is simple: personal customer service and quality work. Careful preparation, correct priming and patient application create the finish that lasts.

Looking for a painter in Limerick for a bedroom, living room, kitchen, hallway, stairway, woodwork or exterior? Send Ahmed the location, a few photographs and a short description. You can also send a room photograph and colour preferences for a digital colour preview before painting begins.
