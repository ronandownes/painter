Frequently asked questions

How far are you prepared to travel?

Tell Ahmed where the job is and he’ll let you know if it is within his normal working area.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp Ahmed with a short description of the job. Photos and the location are useful too.

Can I send photos first?

Yes. Photos are a simple way to show the room, surface or area you want painted before discussing the job.

Who supplies the paint?

Ask Ahmed when you speak. Paint and materials can be discussed for each individual job.

How long will the work take?

That depends on the size of the job and the preparation needed. Ahmed can give you a better idea once he knows what is involved.

What should I send when I contact you?

The location, a few photos and a short description are the easiest starting point.
