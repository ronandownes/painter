PAINTING & DECORATING

AH Painting

Simple painting and decorating, with quality, respect and straightforward communication.

“Quality remains long after cost is forgotten.”

It echoes a well-known maxim associated with Aldo Gucci: “Quality is remembered long after price is forgotten.” The idea is that good workmanship keeps its value after the initial cost has faded from memory.

AH Painting’s idea is simple: good work should respect the person, the home and the finish. Clear communication, careful preparation and dignity in how the job is done matter as much as the final colour.
