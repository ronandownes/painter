AS PAINTING · LIMERICK

Frequently asked questions

Simple answers to the practical questions people usually ask when looking for a painter in Limerick or a nearby area.

How far are you prepared to travel?

AS Painting is focused on Limerick. Send the job location and Ahmed can confirm whether travel to nearby areas such as Shannon, Ennis, Nenagh, Adare, Newcastle West or Thurles makes sense for that job.

How do I get a painting quote?

Call or WhatsApp Ahmed with a short description of the work. The location and a few photographs of the room, wall, exterior or other surface are useful.

Can I send photographs first?

Yes. Photos are an easy way to show the room, exterior or surface before discussing the job and whether a visit is needed.

Who supplies the paint?

Paint and materials can be discussed for each job before anything is bought. The right product depends on the surface, preparation required and the finish you want.

How long will the painting work take?

That depends on the size of the job, the condition of the surfaces and the preparation required. Ahmed can give a better idea once he knows what is involved.

What should I send when I make contact?

The location, a few photographs and a short description are the simplest starting point. If it is an interior job, say which rooms are involved; if it is exterior painting, include a wider photo of the property where possible.
