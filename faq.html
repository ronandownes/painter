AS PAINTING · CAHERDAVIN, LIMERICK

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about working directly with Ahmed on your painting project.

How far are you prepared to travel?

Roughly 50–60 kilometres, or about one hour of driving, depending on the journey.

How do I get a painting quote?

WhatsApp Ahmed on 085 729 6169 with the location, a short description and a few photographs—or simply call.

Will the agreed price change?

No. Clear communication matters: you will know exactly what is included and what you are paying for. Once the work and price are agreed, the price stays fixed. If you request extra work, Ahmed will explain it clearly, quote it separately and wait for your approval before proceeding.

How long will I wait for a reply?

Text messages and voice messages are welcome on WhatsApp. Ahmed may be working and unable to answer a call immediately, but he tries to acknowledge messages as quickly as possible and return calls within 24 hours on working days.

How soon can you start?

Demand varies, but at present Ahmed can usually begin work within one week to ten days.

Can I send photographs first?

Of course. Send the room, exterior or surface on WhatsApp and Ahmed will discuss the next step directly with you.

Who supplies the paint?

Discuss this with Ahmed before anything is bought. The correct product depends on the surface, preparation, priming and finish required.

What does high-quality work mean?

Personal service, careful preparation, correct priming and patient application. Quality remains long after cost is forgotten.

How long will the painting work take?

That depends on the size and condition of the job, especially the preparation required. Ahmed will give a clearer estimate once he sees what is involved.
