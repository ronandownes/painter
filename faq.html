Frequently asked questions

Simple answers to the practical questions people usually ask first.

How far are you prepared to travel?

Tell Ahmed where the job is and he will let you know whether it is within his normal working area.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp Ahmed with a short description of the work. The location and a few photographs are useful.

Can I send photographs first?

Yes. Photos are an easy way to show the room, surface or area before discussing the job.

Who supplies the paint?

Paint and materials can be discussed for each job before anything is bought.

How long will the work take?

That depends on the size of the job and the preparation required. Ahmed can give a better idea once he knows what is involved.

What should I send when I make contact?

The location, a few photographs and a short description are the simplest starting point.
