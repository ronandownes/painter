## Direction for Ahmed's site

The target remains **simpler than most of these sites**:

- One clean page.
- Strong opening line.
- Services immediately understandable.
- A few genuine photos when available.
- Clear Call / WhatsApp contact.
- Short explanation of preparation and workmanship.
- English always available, with multilingual switching.
- No sliders, bouncing sections, excessive animation or crowded menus.
- No competitor links on the live customer-facing page.
