Technology

Tools, methods and practical painting technology.

Tools and methods

Good painting is a combination of preparation, suitable tools and the right application method. The method should suit the surface and the finish required rather than being used simply because it is fashionable.

Technology

Painting technology can include sanding and dust-control systems, spray application, moisture checks and other tools where appropriate. The right method depends on the surface and the job.
