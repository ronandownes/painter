AS PAINTING · LIMERICK

Painting services

Personal, high-quality painting and decorating for homes, based in Caherdavin, Limerick.

Interior house painting

Bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways, stairways, ceilings, walls, doors, skirting, coving and other home decorating. Ahmed discusses the work directly with you and treats your home with care.

Exterior house painting

Exterior masonry, window sills, doors, trim and other outside surfaces. Careful inspection, preparation, correct priming and a suitable exterior paint system are essential to a lasting finish.

Preparation, priming and quality

Quality remains long after cost is forgotten. That quality begins with sound surfaces: cleaning, filling, sanding and priming where required before careful application of the finish coats.

Digital colour preview

Send a photograph of your room and your colour preferences. Ahmed can return digitally edited options showing approximately how the room may look before work begins.

Other painting and decorating work

If your home project does not fit neatly into one heading, ask Ahmed directly. Smaller jobs, rental refreshes, woodwork and complete room updates can all be discussed.

Caherdavin, Limerick and nearby areas

Based in Caherdavin, Ahmed is happy to travel roughly 50–60 kilometres, or about one hour of driving, to work on your project.
