AS PAINTING · LIMERICK

Painting services

Residential interior, residential exterior, commercial and other painting and decorating enquiries in Limerick.

Residential interior painting

Interior painting for bedrooms, living rooms, kitchens, hallways, stairs, ceilings, walls and general home decorating. Tell Ahmed what you want refreshed and what condition the surfaces are in.

Residential exterior painting

Exterior house painting and outside surface enquiries. Surface condition, preparation, weather exposure and the correct exterior paint system all affect the result.

Commercial painting

Shops, offices, rental properties and other commercial painting enquiries can be discussed directly with Ahmed. Timing, access and the type of finish can be planned around the job.

Other painting and decorating work

If the job does not fit neatly into one heading, ask anyway. Smaller jobs, property refreshes, woodwork and other painting or decorating work can be discussed as the business develops.

Limerick and nearby areas

AS Painting is focused on Limerick. For work outside the immediate area, send the location first. Enquiries from Shannon, Ennis, Adare, Croom, Patrickswell, Castleconnell, Nenagh, Newcastle West, Kilmallock and Thurles can be checked individually depending on distance and the size of the job.

The aim is to keep the working area practical rather than promise long-distance travel that makes little sense for either the painter or the customer.
