AH PAINTING

Guidance

A little practical guidance for people planning painting work in their home or property.

Quality

A paint job is not only about price. Preparation, suitable materials, careful application and the way a home is treated all affect how the finished work looks and lasts.

Your home

Residential painting is personal. People may be living, working, caring for children or simply trying to keep everyday life moving while work is happening. Good communication and respect for the space matter.

Colour & finish

You do not need to know every paint term before you call. Explain the room, how it is used and the result you want. Colour, sheen and durability can then be discussed in practical terms.

Preparation

A clean result starts before the first coat. The condition of the surface, filling, sanding, cleaning and any required primer can make a major difference to the finish.
